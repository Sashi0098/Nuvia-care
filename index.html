01 · CHRONIC HEALTH

Non-Communicable Diseases

Learn about common chronic diseases and practical prevention habits.

🩺

Hypertension

High blood pressure that may increase the risk of heart disease and stroke.

  • Regular BP check-up
  • Healthy diet
  • Physical activity
🩸

Diabetes Mellitus

A condition in which blood glucose levels remain high.

  • Monitor blood sugar
  • Balanced diet
  • Regular exercise
⚠️

Risk Factors

Important factors that can affect long-term health.

  • Smoking
  • Unhealthy diet
  • Physical inactivity
  • Obesity

Eye Health & Ophthalmology

Know common eye conditions and the importance of regular eye care.

👁️

Cataract

Clouding of the eye's lens that can affect vision.

👁️

Glaucoma

A group of eye conditions that can damage the optic nerve.

🔎

Blindness Prevention

04 · EMERGENCY AWARENESS

Stroke Awareness

Recognizing warning signs quickly can help someone receive urgent care.

🚨

Warning Signs

Sudden weakness, difficulty speaking, or facial asymmetry may require urgent medical attention.

⚠️

Risk Factors

High blood pressure, diabetes, smoking and other factors can increase risk.

🏥

Immediate Care

Suspected stroke is a medical emergency. Seek immediate professional care.

🚑

Emergency Notice

If someone has severe or sudden symptoms, contact local emergency services or seek immediate medical care.
